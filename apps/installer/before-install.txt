Welcome to VisuSampler!

This installs:

  * VisuMaker  - build your own sample libraries (instruments, interface, effects).
  * VisuPlayer - plays them: a VST3 plugin for your DAW, and a standalone app.

You can pick which parts to install on the next pages.

Where things go
  * VisuMaker and the standalone VisuPlayer: the folder you choose (Program Files by default).
  * The VisuPlayer VST3 plugin: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, where every DAW looks for plugins.
  * Your libraries: Documents\VisuSampler\Libraries (made the first time you start VisuMaker or VisuPlayer).
  * Log files for bug reports: Documents\VisuSampler\Logs.

Uninstalling removes the programs and the plugin, never your libraries, projects or presets.
