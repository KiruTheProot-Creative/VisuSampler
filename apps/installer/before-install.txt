Welcome to the Kiru Creative Audio Suite (Kiru CAS)!

Kiru CAS is the audio side of Kiru's Creative Suite (KCS): plugins and audio tools, in one setup.
This installs:

  * VisuMaker  - build your own sample libraries (instruments, interface, effects).
  * VisuPlayer - plays them: a VST3 plugin for your DAW, and a standalone app.
  * NodeFX     - a node-based effect: cable effects together, 8 macro knobs, .nfx presets.
                 A VST3 plugin and a standalone app.
  * HyperSynth - a two-layer synthesizer (CS-80 style), VST3 plugin and standalone app.

You can pick which parts to install on the next pages.

Where things go
  * VisuMaker and the standalone apps: the folder you choose (Program Files\Kiru CAS by default).
  * The VST3 plugins (VisuPlayer, NodeFX): C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, where every DAW looks for plugins.
  * Your libraries: Documents\VisuSampler\Libraries (made the first time you start VisuMaker or VisuPlayer).
  * NodeFX presets: Documents\VisuSampler\NodeFX Presets.
  * Log files for bug reports: Documents\VisuSampler\Logs.

Uninstalling removes the programs and the plugin, never your libraries, projects or presets.
